## Description

Block trolls, bots and spam on X (Twitter) in one click, and never see them again.

XBlock puts a block button on every post. Click it, and the account is blocked on X and added to your own block list. Share that list with a code, or subscribe to the lists of people you trust: everyone on them is handled for you.

★ ONE-CLICK BLOCKING
• A block button on every post, right in the action bar.
• Pick what it does: Block or Mute on X.
• Pick how it looks: instant, a long-press explosion, or Samurai mode, where you slice the post to pieces with your mouse.
• The shield button does the opposite: that account is never blocked, whatever your lists say.

★ SHARED BLOCK LISTS
• Every account you block goes into your list, with a share code like XBK-7Q2M9.
• Paste someone else's code to subscribe. New entries reach you as they're added.
• Browse a list's accounts before you subscribe.

★ YOUR RULES, LIST BY LIST
• Block on X: blocked for good, they can't see you either.
• Mute on X: gone from your feed, and they'll never know.
• Hide: folded away in your browser, nothing changes on X.
• Blur: blurred in your browser, one click to read it anyway.

★ BATCH BLOCKING
• One click works through every list set to act on X, biggest accounts first.
• One account every 6 to 10 seconds, pausing whenever X asks it to.

★ WORKS IN ANY LANGUAGE
• Blocked someone from X's own menu? XBlock adds them to your list too, whatever language X is in.
• The extension speaks English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Portuguese and Indonesian.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no email, no X password. XBlock makes an anonymous key in your browser.
• Blocks and mutes go from your browser straight to X. We never see your X login.
• No ads, no trackers, no data to sell.

★ FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Every line of the extension and its server is on GitHub, under the MIT License. You can even run your own server.
